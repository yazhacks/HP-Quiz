# HP-Quiz
## Harry Potter general knowledge quiz!! Using html, css, and javascript!
## Includes seven questions all related to the Harry Potter book series.
## Left of question box shows you which of the questions you are on.
## Selecting a correct answer turns it green, selecting a wrong one turns it red!
## After completing the quiz, you'll be able to see your score as well as the correct answer to each question compared to your own answer.
## You get 10 seconds to answer each question with the timer turning red during the final 3 seconds to alert you that you are running out of time!
## You may retake the quiz by selecting the retake option at the end of the quiz.
## Questions and answer choices written by me no AI was used to generate them!
## I had lots of fun with this!!
